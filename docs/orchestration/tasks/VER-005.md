# VER-005 — Recapture the teacher corpus against what the product renders

Status: planned

## Why this task exists

The teacher benchmark scores 62.29 on six measurable cases. Ten turns of
product work raised it from 34.38, and the remaining gap is not in the product:
each unsatisfied claim is bounded by a property of the corpus, measured and
recorded rather than argued.

No task owned this. `VER-001` is `verified` and closed the question of whether
the benchmark is measurable at all; nothing owns the corpus's own correctness.

## What the measurements establish

| Case | Score | Bound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| technical-pdfjs | 90 | Its only evidence passage is 2,478 characters against `MCP-012`'s 1,200-character passages. Two passages joined cannot span it, whatever their order. |
| technical-bun-webview | 86.25 | One claim's patterns spell `backend.url` and `Chrome-only`, strings absent from the page's 27,145 rendered characters. Another's passage is a parameter table captured with cell separators the renderer does not emit. |
| technical-mcp-stdio | 82.5 | Its patterns require the prose "method is `tools/list`" and "result includes a `tools` array". The page states those facts as a JSON request block and never narrates them. |
| technical-sqlite-fts5 | 55 | Two passages are returned; the second one needed ranks 16th at best, because fifteen groups share strictly more of the question's terms. |
| technical-url-canonicalization | 55 | Same shape: the needed passage ranks 13th at best of 104. Its sources are four anchors within one page, which discovery cannot return separately. |
| multilingual-ja-web-standards | 5 | Five of its six accepted sources render; discovery reaches none of them. Reaching them needs `IDNA`, `UTS46`, `ToASCII` — terms neither the question nor any reachable page supplies. |

[ADR-0016](../adr/0016-corpus-patterns-do-not-match-captured-passages.md)
records the general form: **no claim in the corpus has a captured passage that
satisfies its own `acceptable_patterns` — 0 of 8.** A product returning the
exact span the corpus points at fails the corpus's own check.

## Scope

1. Derive each claim's `acceptable_patterns` from its captured passage rather
   than authoring them beside it, and verify every pattern against its own
   passage before sealing. `benchmarks/grader/corpus-self-consistency.test.ts`
   already counts this and currently records zero.
2. Recapture passages against what the product renders. The current captures
   came from page HTML; the grader now compensates with four normalisation
   rules — whitespace, pre-punctuation space, backticks, quote spacing — each
   added because a capture and a render differed by characters no reader sees.
   A capture taken from the rendered text needs none of them.
3. Split or reject captures longer than `MCP-012`'s passage size. The PDF.js
   capture is unreachable by construction and should not be scored as a product
   failure.
4. Decide what to do with claims whose patterns quote prose the page does not
   contain. Rewriting them to match the page is corpus work; leaving them is a
   permanent floor.

## Out of scope

Passage selection. [ADR-0015](../adr/0015-lexical-passage-selection-limit.md)
establishes arithmetically that lexical ranking cannot reach the two cases at
55, for the single passage and for the pair, and records eleven measured
alternatives that did not clear that bound. Changing it means selecting a
passage by what it is about, which is a decision about
[ADR-0006](../adr/0006-codex-only-teacher-with-deterministic-grounding.md)'s
deterministic boundary rather than corpus work.

## Acceptance

`corpus-self-consistency.test.ts` records a non-zero count of claims whose
captured passage satisfies its own patterns, and the recorded score is
re-measured against an empty workspace so it is not a warm-cache artefact —
the procedure
[ADR-0013](../adr/0013-measurable-teacher-benchmark-with-captured-passages.md)
now documents.

`gates_release` stays `false` until this task is verified, as
[ADR-0010](../adr/0010-defer-teacher-benchmark-release-gating.md) requires.
